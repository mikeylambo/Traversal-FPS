# Traversal Platform System — reference analysis

Reference: `fedc2256-9e06-450c-ae27-a7d21829957d.png` (1448 x 1086)

## Identification

The reference is a concept sheet for a modular mechanical platform family. The reconstruction target is the reusable standard moving deck, with optional lift-guide presentation parts. Primary domain: `object`. Classification confidence: 0.98.

## Overall form and silhouette

The standard deck is a shallow, bilaterally symmetric cuboid with a rectangular footprint approximately 2.1 times wider than its depth and roughly 0.18 times as thick as its depth. Its silhouette is layered: a thin off-white upper shell sits above a darker, inset undercarriage. Corners are chamfered rather than square. The lift variant places the same deck above a square base using a central telescoping rectangular column.

## Component hierarchy

- Macro: upper deck shell, graphite undercarriage, optional lift column/base.
- Meso: four corner caps, perimeter fascia panels, underside support cassette, cyan emitter rails, top panel quadrants, lift guide channels.
- Micro: recessed fasteners, narrow seam lines, corner blocks, small warning/logo plaques, surface scuffs, cyan corner nodes.

## Materials and surface

- Upper shell: opaque coated dielectric, cool off-white albedo, metalness about 0.05, roughness 0.48–0.62, shallow panel relief and sparse edge wear.
- Undercarriage/corner hardware: opaque graphite metal/polymer mix, low-value neutral albedo, metalness 0.45–0.75, roughness 0.28–0.46.
- Cyan emitters: emissive translucent/opaque light strips behind dark recesses; high saturation cyan, bloom-facing intensity, low roughness.
- Seams/fasteners: dark neutral metal, slightly lower roughness than the painted shell.

## Color and finish

The finish is a high-value cool neutral deck against a near-black graphite chassis. Cyan is restricted to thin functional rails and guide strips. The shell is satin rather than glossy. Panel faces vary subtly in value, and wear is sparse: fine gray scuffs and lightly darkened seams, not heavy grime.

## Identity-defining features

1. Thin white deck over a visibly inset dark mechanical chassis.
2. Chamfered corners with separate dark corner cap hardware.
3. Long recessed cyan fascia emitters plus smaller corner nodes.
4. Four-panel top layout with a central seam cross and perimeter seam.
5. Small triangular Traversal mark centered on a fascia panel.
6. Optional lift column with paired vertical cyan guide rails.

## Uncertainty and single-image limits

The concept board repeats several perspectives but does not provide clean orthographic source images. The rear fascia is partially visible and can be mirrored with high confidence; the exact underside mechanism, back of the lift guide, internal rail construction, and real material microstructure are inferred. The reconstruction should preserve the readable design grammar rather than claim manufacturing-accurate geometry. Existing gameplay collision remains authoritative.
